
    ==================== SLIDE 9: KEY INSIGHTS ====================
    💡 What We Learned:
    
    1. POSITIVE FINDINGS:
       • Privacy and accuracy CAN coexist
       • DP implementation adds minimal overhead
       • Models inherently resist some attacks through generalization
       • Hate speech detection doesn't heavily memorize data
    
    2. CHALLENGES:
       • Target accuracy (95%) not fully reached (82% achieved)
       • Manual DP implementation due to library issues
       • Computational constraints for larger models
    
    3. SURPRISES:
       • Baseline model already had good privacy properties
       • Attack resistance was better than expected
