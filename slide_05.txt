
    ==================== SLIDE 5: DIFFERENTIAL PRIVACY ====================
    🔒 How DP Works:
    
    1. Add calibrated noise during training
    2. Clip gradients to bound sensitivity
    3. Provide mathematical privacy guarantees
    
    📊 Our Implementation:
    • ε (epsilon): Privacy budget (lower = more private)
    • δ (delta): Probability of privacy failure
    • Noise multiplier: Controls noise amount
    • Result: (ε≈5.0, δ=1e⁻⁵)-DP guarantee
    
    💡 Key Insight:
    Individual data points cannot be identified in output
